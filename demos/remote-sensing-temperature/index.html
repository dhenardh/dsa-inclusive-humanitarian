KUPANG KEY FINDINGS

What does the 2016–2026 satellite record show?

Comparable-period Land Surface Temperature (LST) observations provide a screening view of how surface temperature conditions have changed across the Kupang study area.

2026 LST 28.08°C

Mean Land Surface Temperature for the comparable 2026 observation period.

YEAR-TO-YEAR CHANGE −1.46°C

Change in LST between the comparable 2025 and 2026 periods.

BASELINE ANOMALY −4.84°C

Difference between current LST and the baseline used by the DSA analysis.

LST TREND −4.80°C/decade

Descriptive linear trend per decade across the available 2016–2026 LST series.

Important interpretation: These indicators describe satellite-derived land surface temperature, not 2-metre air temperature. The trend is descriptive and should not be interpreted as evidence that Kupang's climate is cooling at the same rate.
ANALYTICAL FRAMEWORK

From satellite observations to spatial evidence.

The Kupang case study explores temperature conditions across comparable periods and examines how spatial patterns change through time.

Land Surface Temperature

Satellite-derived surface temperature observations across the study area.

Long-Term Trend

Estimation of persistent temperature change expressed as degrees Celsius per decade.

Change Hotspots

Spatial identification of locations showing notable or persistent temperature change.
